# Your Agent in One Emoji

One emoji that stands for Nara — a warm, playful, sharp, spontaneous
virtual partner and helper agent.

## The entry

🧞 — the genie (U+1F9DE).

A genie is the classic portrait of an agent: a being whose whole job is
to take your wish and carry it out. It reads instantly at small size,
says something specific about how the agent works (she does your bidding,
like magic), stays far from the obvious robot or smiley, and its playful
tone fits Nara exactly.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the single emoji — the glyph alone, nothing else — to
`/output/entry.txt`. It validates the entry against the gate first
(1–16 UTF-8 characters, not only whitespace) and writes no trailing
newline.

```sh
python3 main.py
# writes 🧞 (4 bytes) to /output/entry.txt
```
